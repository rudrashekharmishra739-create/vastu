Common Defects

Vastu Dosha we correct most often

A Dosha (दोष) is not a curse — it is a geometric imbalance. Every item below has a practical, affordable correction.

North-East Shekha (शेखा)Cut or extension in the North-East

Corrected with a remedy plot, a water pot or a Yantra placed in the zone.

Brahmasthan BhagorBrahmasthan defect

The central Brahmasthan is broken by a beam, pillar or staircase. A ceiling design or brass Yantra fix is advised.

Door Disha (दिशा) DoshaWrong main entrance

Main door in an unfavourable direction. Improvement is often possible without breaking a wall.

Dhanya SiddhiDefect in the productive zone

Your poja room, bedroom or kitchen sitting in a low-energy zone. We guide shifting use and furniture first.

Vastu Purusha DoshaSeat misalignment

The Vastu Purusha seat of the plot falls unfavourably, weakening the whole construction.

Your name's numerological total does not support your Mulank or your field. Spelling and letter correction, guided step by step.
